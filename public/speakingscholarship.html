Augustine Udefa Speaking Scholarship

The Augustine Udefa Speaking Scholarship is awarded to students in African educational institutions who can demonstrate good public speaking skills and critical thinking ability. The scholarship is open to students in any discipline. Award recipients must demonstrate good moral conduct and succeed in a series of speaking competitions. Scholarship support and cash prizes vary by competition position.

Eligibility

  • You must be a student in an African public university, polytechnic, monotechnic, or college of education in your second, third, or fourth year.
  • You must be of good moral conduct.
  • You must submit all required application documents via the application portal. Documents required include curriculum vitae, admission letter, and an attestation letter from your Head of Department (HOD) on their letterhead, attesting to your good moral conduct.

Scholarship Value

  • Winner:
    • Full school fee support for the period of your program plus a cash prize of eighty dollars ($80) for school stationery.
    • Global recognition.
  • Second Position:
    • Full school fee support for one academic session, plus a cash prize of forty dollars ($40) for school stationery.
    • Global recognition.
  • Third Position:
    • Full school fee support for one academic session.
    • Global recognition.

Renewal & Termination

Renewal

The winner of the Augustine Udefa Speaking Scholarship Competition is eligible for scholarship renewal every academic session. Renewal is contingent on fulfilling all of the following conditions:

  • Maintain a minimum cumulative grade point average (CGPA) of 4.0 on a 5.0 scale or 3.0 on a 4.0 scale.
  • Submit a letter from your Head of Department (HOD) confirming your CGPA and ability to complete your studies on time.
  • Submit two attestation letters from academic referees confirming your good moral conduct.

Termination

Recipients are expected to be good ambassadors of their institutions and people of good moral values. Your scholarship will be terminated if:

  • You do not meet the minimum CGPA requirement for renewal.
  • You are rusticated from your institution because of misconduct of any form.
  • You are found making or sharing social media posts that promote nudity and sexual immorality.

Application Requirements

Complete the application form and upload all required documents. Required documents include:

  • A cover letter expressing your interest in the AUSS competition and why you should be considered.
  • Curriculum vitae (CV).
  • Admission letter.
  • Attestation letter from your Head of Department (HOD).

Speaking Competition

The speaking competition will commence in January 2027. Shortlisted applicants will be selected to participate in the speaking competition. The applicants will be divided into groups with a specific number of speakers in each group. They will be assigned topics to speak on, and a winner will be selected by majority vote from the public and scores from a panel of independent judges. The speaking competition will take place on our social media channels dedicated specifically for this purpose:

  • Facebook: Y’tino Intellectual Discourse
  • Instagram: ytino2025
  • TikTok: Y’tino Intellectual Discourse
  • YouTube: Augustine Udefa Foundation

Each speaker will make a video presentation of their points and forward the video to the Foundation via a dedicated email address which will be sent to the speakers. The video should be clear and audible and not be more than 3 minutes.

Stages

  • Stage 1 - Knockout: Shortlisted applicants will be assigned to five groups. In each group, speakers will be paired to compete. Speakers who lose a competition and have low points will be knocked out immediately. Winners will be paired to compete again, and losing speakers will continue to be knocked out until only the speaker with the highest points remains in each group.
  • Stage 2 - Semi-Finals: The five winners from Stage 1 (one from each group) will compete among themselves. The two speakers with the highest points will proceed to the grand finale. The speaker with the third-highest points will emerge as second runner-up. If three speakers tie in points, all three will proceed to the grand finale.
  • Stage 3 - Grand Finale: The finalists will compete to win the Augustine Udefa Speaking Scholarship. Scholarships and cash prizes will be awarded as described above.

Voting and Scoring

A panel of independent judges will listen to the content of your presentation and score you out of 70% according to a set of scoring criteria. Votes from the public will constitute 30% of your total score.
