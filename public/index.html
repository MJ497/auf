About the Foundation

Our Mission

The Augustine Udefa Foundation is committed to improving the lives of people, especially those with the fewest resources, by providing opportunities for them to access free education and quality health services.

Our Vision

The Foundation envisages a future where free educational institutions are established, and health services provided free of charge to the less privileged and financially disadvantaged people in our society. We believe that every life is important, and education is the bedrock of a nation. So, everyone, including the poor, should have the privilege to access health care and the formal education they desire and need.

Augustine Udefa

Founder & Chairman

Augustine Udefa is an accomplished educator, researcher, and philanthropist dedicated to advancing human health and educational equity.

His academic journey began in Nigeria, where he attended Command Children’s School in Calabar for his elementary education. He commenced his secondary education at Stella Maris Seminary in Uyanga and completed it at St. Patrick’s College in Calabar. Augustine subsequently earned his Bachelor of Science and Master of Science degrees in Physiology, alongside a Postgraduate Diploma in Education, from the University of Calabar. Continuing his pursuit of academic excellence, he is currently advancing his research as a doctoral scholar in Nutrition and Exercise Physiology.

Driven by a profound commitment to service, Augustine approaches philanthropy with the same rigor and dedication that define his scientific career. He established the Augustine Udefa Foundation out of a deep-rooted love for humanity and a core conviction: that the world becomes a better place when privileged individuals extend a helping hand to those in need.

His burning desire to serve led to the creation of the Foundation to ensure that people, especially those with the fewest resources, gain meaningful access to free education and quality health services.

Sunday Okpa

Sunday Okpa serves as the Secretary of the Augustine Udefa Foundation, bringing a rich background in clinical research coordination, health data analytics, and physiological sciences to the organization's leadership team.

Sunday currently serves as the Site Coordinator for the International Centre for the Study of Breast Cancer Subtypes (ICSBCS) at the University of Calabar Teaching Hospital (UCTH) study site. As a skilled REDCap administrator and data analyst, he has transformed site operations, driving an impressive increase of over 80% in patient enrollment and overall research output at the UCTH site. His commitment to global health equity is further reflected in his participation in the 7th and 8th Annual ICSBCS Symposia on improving gynecological cancer management in low- and middle-income countries, hosted in Calabar, Nigeria, and Kumasi, Ghana.

Sunday holds both a Bachelor of Science and a Master of Science in Physiology from the University of Calabar, Cross River State, Nigeria. Passionate about genomics and its role in understanding breast cancer subtypes, he combines analytical expertise with a dedicated humanitarian focus to help guide the Foundation's healthcare initiatives and operations.

Scholarship Programs

We offer comprehensive support to students at all academic levels.

AUSS — Augustine Udefa Speaking Scholarship

The Augustine Udefa Speaking Scholarship is awarded to students in African educational institutions, who can demonstrate good public speaking skills and critical thinking ability.

  • Full school fee support for the period of your program.
Apply now

AUOS — Augustine Udefa Orphan Scholarship

The Augustine Udefa Orphan Scholarship is awarded to people who have lost both parents and do not have any sponsor to support their education

  • For Secondary & university students
  • minimum cumulative grade point average (CGPA) of 3.5 on a 5.0 scale or 2.5 on a 4.0 scale
Apply now

AUES — Augustine Udefa Excellence Scholarship

The Augustine Udefa Excellence Scholarship is a highly competitive scholarship awarded to students with excellent academic profile.

  • Minimum CGPA of 4.30 out of 5.0 or 3.30 out of 4.0
  • Full school fees coverage + stipend
Apply now

AUSMP — Augustine Udefa Scholarship Mentorship Program

Mentorship & full-scholarship preparation for excellent but underfunded applicants from Africa seeking graduate study in the United States. .

  • Application deadline:Applications are accepted all year round on a rolling basis.
  • Mentorship & preparation: Until a fully funded scholarship is secured
  • Scholarship start: Fall or Spring semester (depending on award).
